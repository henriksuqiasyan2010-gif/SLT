# SLT — results

All numbers below are generated by `scripts/make_team_kit.py` from the result files.

## Model

- Task: recognise one of **250 isolated ASL signs** from MediaPipe landmarks (dataset: Google — Isolated Sign Language Recognition, Kaggle; 94,477 clips from 21 people).
- Model: **Transformer**, 1.9 M parameters, input 32 frames x 542 features, best of 100 epochs (epoch 88). Runs on CPU with ONNX Runtime in about 1-3 ms per sign.
- Validation: **4 people the model never saw** (18,528 clips; 75,949 clips of the other 17 people for training; GroupKFold(5) by participant, fold 0).

## Accuracy on people the model never saw

| | top-1 | top-3 |
|---|---|---|
| Model (`main-reg30`) | **62.7%** | **76.9%** |
| Same, video thinned to 13 fps (a slow laptop) | 59.6% | 74.5% |
| App pipeline (second-hand rule on) | **65.1%** | — |
| App pipeline at 13 fps | 61.7% | — |

top-1 = the right sign is the first answer; top-3 = it is among the three answers the app shows. Chance level is 0.4%.

The second-hand rule: when both hands are visible and one of them stays still, the still hand is ignored. On the 1,833 validation clips with both hands in a frame it raises top-1 from 36.7% to 61.3%.

Per person (app pipeline): 27610: 71.2%, 28656: 68.2%, 29302: 57.0%, 49445: 64.7%.

Presentation-only number, **not a model metric**: if near-synonyms count as correct (awake/wake, give/gift, goose/duck, kitty/cat, pencil/pen, mouth/lips, listen/hear), top-1 is 64.1%.

## How we got there (top-1, same validation people)

| Step | top-1 | top-3 |
|---|---|---|
| 1D-CNN baseline | 52.6% | 69.3% |
| Transformer + augmentation | 56.8% | 72.9% |
| + regularisation, 100 epochs | 62.3% | 77.1% |
| + 30 augmented versions | 62.7% | 76.9% |
| + second-hand rule (app) | 65.1% | — |

Tried and not kept: an ensemble of three models (+1.1 pp, three times the compute); the second-hand rule inside training (64.4%, below the app pipeline's 65.1%).

## Tests with the author's own signing (webcam of the laptop)

- Recorded single signs: top-1 correct in **21 of 24**; misses: dad (not in top-3), dad (rank 3), mom (rank 3). The dad recordings were made before the author learned the sign in Learn mode (the thumb must touch the forehead); afterwards dad was recognised in 3 of 4 attempts.
- First test in the app (10 different signs, one attempt each): **10 of 10** correct.
- Sentences (3 recorded sessions of 5 signs, automatic segmentation): **15 of 15** words correct, 15 segments found.

## Speed (HP laptop, Intel Core i3-1215U, no GPU)

- Camera + MediaPipe Holistic + interface: 16-25 frames per second (median 16 after 5 minutes of continuous use; the CPU slows down when it gets warm).
- Recognition of one sign (clean-up + features + model): 2-5 ms.

## Limits

- Isolated signs only: the app recognises single signs (or a sequence of single signs with pauses), it does not translate grammatical ASL sentences.
- 250 everyday signs (the dataset's vocabulary), not the whole of ASL.
- Accuracy depends on the person: 57-71% across the four held-out people.
- Fingerspelling, facial grammar and fast continuous signing are not covered.
